# Governance

Keelson is maintained by Salvatore Miseo (GitLab: salvo8519), who decides on the roadmap,
reviews security-tier changes and publishes releases.

Decisions that change the structure of the template are discussed in an issue before the
merge request. Contributors who keep a steady record of reviewed contributions may be invited
to become maintainers.

Contact: through a confidential issue on GitLab.
