Changing a knowledge base needs write access, not read access: the application's knowledge bases are changed by platform administrators only, an organization's by its owners, admins and members (viewers read), in the service and in row level security. Deleting a document or a knowledge base removes its vectors, a delete that removes nothing answers 404, collection names are unique, and local sync reads only inside `RAG_LOCAL_SYNC_ROOT` into the application's knowledge bases.
