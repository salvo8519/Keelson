Refresh tokens are rotated with reuse detection and signing out ends every token of the session, an impersonation ends when its administrator loses the role or signs out everywhere, a present `Origin` header (`null` included) decides alone on cookie requests, and the session version can no longer decrease. An e-mail address changes only through a link sent to the new address (`POST /users/me/email`; `PATCH /users/me` no longer accepts `email`), so single sign-on can no longer take over an account moved onto an unconfirmed address; users sign in again once after the upgrade.
